Cards = cards in that slice carrying the tag. The universe is the whole draftable set: 280 cards, collector numbers 1–280 (1–194 are the main set and 195–280 the Echoed Pairs; both come out of the same draft boosters (three Echoed cards per pack), so they are counted alike); basic lands are excluded. Share = cards ÷ size of the slice. Lift = share ÷ the set-wide share: 1.0× is no more than anywhere else, 2× twice as concentrated, 3×+ is where the theme lives. Tables rank by lift unless you switch them to card count above or click a column header; each shows its top 12 with show more underneath.

Every slice is by colour identity. A colour is its mono-coloured cards (id=w). A pair is shown two ways side by side: A&B = the cards that are exactly both colours (the gold and hybrid signposts, id=wu), and A∪B = the pair's whole pool — mono cards of either colour plus those A&B cards (id<=wu -id:c) — because that is what a two-colour deck actually draws from. The set-wide bars split each tag's cards the same way: mono W–G, colourless (C), exactly each pair, three-plus colours (Multi) — they add up to the Cards column. Tags on fewer than 3 cards (2 for an A&B slice) are dropped: one card is not a theme.

Structural tags (triggered-ability, alliteration, modal…) are left out of every table until you tick the box above; set-cycle tags have their own section. Tags are community-applied, so an absent tag means “not tagged yet”, not “not in the set”. Scryfall's otag: search also matches child tags and back faces, so a search can return a few more cards than the count here. The ? after each tag opens its definition on the Tagger. Hover a card name for its image; click it for its Scryfall page.
