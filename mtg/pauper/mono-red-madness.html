Slot by slot

75 slots, shaded by how many decks hold them

Every card copy in the stock list gets one tile, grouped by card, with the average copies across the field printed after the name. A tile goes dark when nearly every deck plays at least that many copies, and heats up towards red as the field thins out — the ember tiles are the slots actually being argued over. Cards that miss the stock list but that a real slice of the field still plays are drawn after it in dashed outlines: slots competing to get in, rather than slots being defended.

Fingerprints

All decks, one row each

Rows are decks, sorted by how far they sit from the stock list. Columns are the cards that anyone changed. Warm cells are extra copies, cool cells are cuts, blank means the deck agrees with the stock list. The mainboard block is nearly empty; the sideboard block is static.

Distance

How many cards you would have to change

Distance is the number of card slots between a deck and the stock list — swap one Mountain for one Faithless Looting and you are at 1.
