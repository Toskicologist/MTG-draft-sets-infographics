Set reviews, compared

Shadows over Innistrad came out in 2016, four years before 17Lands, so there is no win-rate data for it. Instead this table compares three set reviews from the time that gave a number to almost every card: LSV (ChannelFireball), MtG Grinders and Draftsim. Each reviewer's scores are turned into z-scores (0 = that reviewer's average card, +1 = one standard deviation better), so reviewers with different habits can be averaged fairly. Avg norm is the mean of a card's z-scores; Controversy is how much the reviewers disagree (standard deviation of their z-scores).

Sources, method and caveats
  • LSV: Luis Scott-Vargas's Limited set review on ChannelFireball, March 2016, read from the Internet Archive. Scale 0-5. Where he gives two numbers ("1.0 // 3.5" = normally // in the right deck) the first is used. He rated the ten dual lands once, as a cycle (3.0). All 282 cards rated.
  • MtG Grinders: the team's set review on the Tyrennii blog, March-April 2016, scale 0-5, one reviewer per color (white David Lane, blue Trent Clarke, black Coen Malone, red Lewis Adams, green Jordon Emmett, gold/artifacts/lands Michael Barker). Ranges such as "2.5-3" score their midpoint. Stormrider Spirit is left blank: its review printed "Construed 3.0 / Limited 0.0", which looks like swapped labels. 269 cards rated.
  • Draftsim: the ratings Draftsim's draft simulator bots use for SOI, 0-5. They do not cover the 33 double-faced cards or Ulvenwald Mysteries. 248 cards rated.
  • Hu pick: Haibing Hu's pick order for commons and uncommons (Galactic Treasures), as a rank within its color and rarity (W-C #1 = best white common). It is a ranking, not a rating, so it is shown but not averaged.
  • The 17Lands, EDHREC rank and Price columns use the same green-to-red bands as the reviews, from each column's own z-score (EDHREC rank and price on a log scale, since a few cards dwarf the rest).
  • EDHREC rank (lower = played in more Commander decks) and Price (USD, TCGplayer market price of the SOI printing, non-foil) come from Scryfall, snapshot .
  • Card names in the reviews were matched to Scryfall by hand where reviewers misspelled them; nothing was guessed. Raw pages and the parser are in the project's research folder.
  • Not used: Draftaholics Anonymous (a 2016 community pick-order site) is offline; Terrible Magic's reviews name cards only in images; Limited Resources' review is audio only.
  • 17Lands (SIR) GIH WR: games-in-hand win rate from 17Lands, Premier Draft, all time (fetched ), for Shadows over Innistrad Remastered (Arena, 2023). That is a different format: it shares 153 of these 282 cards, fills the rest mostly with Eldritch Moon cards plus a bonus sheet of older Innistrad cards, and changed 28 rarities. So it is real play data, but for a different environment: shown for comparison, never averaged. n/a = card not in SIR. Hover a cell for SIR rarity, sample size and average pick seen.
