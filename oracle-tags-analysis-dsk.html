Cards = cards in that slice carrying the tag. The universe is the whole draftable set: 271 cards, collector numbers 1–271 (every card numbered below the set's first basic land, i.e. the cards found in its draft boosters); basic lands are excluded. the small figure beside it is its share = cards ÷ size of the slice. Lift = share ÷ the set-wide share: 1.0× is no more than anywhere else, 2× twice as concentrated, 3×+ is where the theme lives. Tables rank by lift unless you switch them to card count above or click a column header; each shows its top 12 with show more underneath.

Every slice is by color identity. A color is its mono-colored cards (id=w). A pair is shown two ways side by side: A&B = the cards that are exactly both colors (the gold and hybrid signposts, id=wu), and A∪B = the pair's whole pool — mono cards of either color plus those A&B cards (id<=wu -id:c) — because that is what a two-color deck actually draws from; each pair row's bars show the A∪B count (plain) and its split into mono A, mono B and A&B; click a bar label to sort by it. Pair tables rank by A&B lift by default, tags that clear the A&B floor first; cards after a tag lists its cards (with [Show images] at the end). The set-wide bars split each tag's cards the same way: mono W–G, colorless (C), exactly each pair, three-plus colors (Multi) — they add up to the Cards column. Tags on fewer than 3 cards (2 for an A&B slice or colorless) are dropped: one card is not a theme.

Structural tags (triggered-ability, alliteration, modal…) are left out of every table until you tick Include structural tags above (off by default); Show cards there opens every visible row's card list at once; set-cycle tags have their own section. Tags are community-applied, so an absent tag means “not tagged yet”, not “not in the set”. Scryfall's otag: search also matches child tags and back faces, so a search can return a few more cards than the count here. The ? after each tag opens its definition on the Tagger. Hover a card name for its image; click it for its Scryfall page.
